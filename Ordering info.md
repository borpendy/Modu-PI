The Modu-PI mainboard is a 4 layer, impedance controlled board. This must be ordered from a PCB fabricator, and I used and recommend JLCPCB for this.
To ensure all trace impedances are correct, for the usb and display lines, you **must** order the board with the **JLC04161H-7628** layer stackup, AND **1.6 mm** thickness. Get assembly on the top side only, as most components used are SMD, and hard to hand solder. My recommended settings for jlcpcb are shown below:
